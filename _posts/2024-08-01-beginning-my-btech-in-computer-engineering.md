## Transitioning to Engineering

Beginning my B.Tech in Computer Engineering marked the transition from viewing programming simply as academic coursework to approaching software engineering as a disciplined, long-term craft.

The early semesters focused on establishing solid technical foundations through C, Python, Java, databases, and operating system principles, alongside deepening my practical immersion in Linux and Git.

Outside formal syllabi, I began experimenting with self-hosting, personal command-line utilities, and network reverse proxies. These early experiments clarified my primary technical inclination: understanding backend systems, operating system internals, and network architecture rather than superficial frontend assembly.

Although my early software was simple, this period laid the foundational habits upon which every subsequent project and architectural milestone was built.
