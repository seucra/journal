# About this journal

I’m **Shams Tabrez Ahmed (Seucra)**, a Computer Engineering student in Mumbai. This is my home for original technical writing: notes from projects, explanations I wish I had found earlier, and honest records of what I am still learning.

My current interests include Rust, backend systems, Linux, Matrix-based communication, self-hosted infrastructure, cybersecurity fundamentals, and vulnerability prioritization.

The companion site, [seucra.tech](https://seucra.tech), is my engineering portfolio. It holds project, infrastructure, research, and timeline documentation; this journal is where I expand on the decisions and lessons behind that work.

## What to expect

- Project logs and postmortems for work I can explain and defend.
- Rust, Matrix, WebAssembly, Linux, and infrastructure learning notes.
- Cybersecurity and research notes that distinguish experiments from completed work.
- No fabricated expertise, generic AI tutorials, or claims beyond the evidence.

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/seucra) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/s-t-ahmed/), or contact me through the links on [seucra.tech](https://seucra.tech/contact/).
